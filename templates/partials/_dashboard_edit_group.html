General
Triggers in this group

Tick a trigger to put it in this group (it's saved straight away). A trigger can be in several groups.

{# ─────────── NOTIFIERS (the group's alerts) ─────────── #}
Notifiers
Sent when any of this group's triggers fire

Each alert that's on goes out once per call and names the group's triggers that fired, for example Station 12, Station 14 in {trigger_list} (and the group in {group_name}). A trigger with its own alert on a channel sends that one instead. Blank tokens and messages use the system's. The system's alert is separate: turn a trigger's Include in the system's alert off if it should only go out through its group.
